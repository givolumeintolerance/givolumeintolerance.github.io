What is GI volume intolerance?

GI volume intolerance describes difficulty tolerating the volume of food or fluid in the gastrointestinal tract. For some people, even relatively small amounts can trigger symptoms such as nausea, abdominal pain, pressure or discomfort.

The term is not currently an established medical diagnosis. This project aims to bring together people with similar experiences and explore what we can learn from each other and from existing research.
